# Archive

Older plans and notes, kept for the record. They are not up to date, and they keep the old name, SwarmLLM, on purpose: that is what the project was called when they were written. For current docs, see [../README.md](../README.md).

- [master-plan.md](master-plan.md): the first long-form plan behind the roadmap.
- [roadmap-review.md](roadmap-review.md): the gap review that produced roadmap items 12 to 23.
- [kernel-plan.md](kernel-plan.md), [kernel-plan-2.md](kernel-plan-2.md), [kernel-plan-3.md](kernel-plan-3.md): kernel plans, most of which shipped (see [../kernels.md](../kernels.md)).
- [deltanet-prefill-research.md](deltanet-prefill-research.md): the research behind the register-resident DeltaNet kernels (the spec is current: [../deltanet-prefill-spec.md](../deltanet-prefill-spec.md)).
- [research/](research/): early research notes on decode overhead and the wire, exact forward-pass ideas, a Mac and Metal plan, and the network scheduler. The prefill GEMM v2 design is current, in [../research/prefill-gemm-v2.md](../research/prefill-gemm-v2.md).
- [handoff/](handoff/): handoff notes between work sessions.
